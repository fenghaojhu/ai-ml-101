# Module 02: Math for learning

**Week(s):** 2–3  
**Learning goal:** Use vectors, dot products, functions, derivatives and probabilities to explain a prediction.

## Learn

A vector is an ordered list of numbers. A dot product multiplies matching entries and sums them; weighted sums are the basis of many models. A derivative measures local change, and a gradient collects derivatives. A probability describes uncertainty on a 0–1 scale; it is not a guarantee.

## Worked example

For features [2, 3] and weights [0.5, -1], the dot product is -2. A small change to the first feature changes the output by 0.5 times that change.
